3. TOURS, PLANNING AND BOOKING

Support two tour entry paths: booking a published tour package, or submitting a custom trip built from configured tourism services. Both paths end in the same optional accommodation-preferences section and a final review before submission.


Published tour packages

Display tour name, region, image, duration, daily itinerary, destinations, inclusions, exclusions, price, currency and price basis. Book Now carries the tour ID and selections into the form. Validate travel dates, adult/child quantities and published status on the server. Clearly identify services already included in the package.


Custom trip sequence

1. Choose Zanzibar or Tanzania Mainland, travel dates and party size. One trip uses one region in the initial version.

2. Select compatible destinations and optional food options, activities and guide services. Show units, quantities, durations and an updating estimated subtotal.

3. Review the tour services and provide traveler contact details.

4. At the end of the booking form, offer Do you need help booking accommodation? Default to No. Expand the preferences fields only when Yes is selected.

5. Review the full request, including an accommodation summary where requested, complete anti-bot verification and submit once.

Remove the source planner's direct hotel-selection step and selectable standalone transport step. Transport uses its own menu and booking flow. Transport already included within a tour remains part of that package's stated inclusions; do not create a separate route booking or add another transport charge automatically.

Booking field | Requirement

- Full name / email / phone: Required; allow international phone formats.

- Country of residence: Required.

- Start date / end date or duration: Required as applicable; end cannot precede start. Reject past service dates/times and validate tour constraints in the service timezone.

- Adults / children / child ages: At least one adult; collect child ages only when pricing or accommodation needs them.

- Region / selected services / estimate: Generated from verified server data, not trusted hidden prices.

- Preferred contact / special requests: Email or supported phone/WhatsApp preference; bounded optional text.

- Privacy acknowledgement / terms: Required acknowledgement or consent as appropriate; marketing permission is separate and optional.

On success, save a unique booking reference and show Request received, subject to availability and final confirmation. Email the customer an acknowledgement and notify the administrator. No customer account is required. A booking reference by itself must not grant access to personal details.


4. ACCOMMODATION PREFERENCE FIELDS

Accommodation is a service arranged by a guide after a tour request. The customer describes the desired stay rather than selecting a named hotel or seeing live hotel availability. Keep this section optional and easy to complete.

Field | Behaviour when accommodation is requested

- Accommodation type: Hotel, resort, boutique hotel, guesthouse, apartment, villa or Not sure; options are administrator-managed.

- Class / star preference: Budget, mid-range, luxury or Not sure; optional 3/4/5-star preference. Categories are configurable.

- Preferred location: Configured region-compatible area plus an Other / describe location text box. Include Not sure.

- Check-in / check-out: Required; check-out must be after check-in. Calculate nights from dates. Default sensibly from the tour but allow stays before or after it.

- Guests: Adults and children; default from the tour and allow a different accommodation party. Collect child ages where relevant.

- Rooms / room type: Positive room count; single, double, twin, family or flexible. Validate counts; the guide confirms actual occupancy suitability.

- Budget and currency: Optional budget per room per night, explicitly labelled; TZS or USD. Budget is a preference, not a confirmed price.

- Meal plan: Room only, breakfast, half board, full board, all inclusive or Not sure.

- Useful preferences: Optional beachfront, pool, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, accessible room or similar configurable choices.

- Additional details: A multiline text box for location details, requirements and other useful instructions; enforce a server-side length limit.


Display and validation rules

- Show a concise message: Tell us your preferences. Our guide will find suitable options and send you the price and payment details.

- Only check-in/out, guest counts and room count must become required after Yes is selected. Type, class and location may use Not sure so the form remains simple.

- If the customer switches to No, exclude previously entered accommodation values from the final booking and ignore them server-side.

- Show the tour estimate separately from Accommodation: Pending quotation. Do not display a zero hotel cost or label the subtotal as a complete trip price.

- A preferences request does not reserve a room. Store requested amenities as preferences for the guide to verify.

- Do not collect passport scans, card numbers or unnecessary sensitive information in this initial form. A guide can request essential supplier details later through an appropriate private process.

A standalone Transport booking must not reveal this accommodation form or depend on accommodation, tour or trip-plan records. If a tour already includes a hotel, clearly explain the existing inclusion and have the guide treat further accommodation preferences as an amendment rather than charge for the same stay twice.


5. GUIDE SOURCING AND HOTEL RESERVATION

The assigned guide or authorized administrator reviews accommodation preferences, uses hotels they know or other suitable suppliers, checks availability and records the hotel arrangement privately in the dashboard.

1. Open the tour request and its accommodation preferences. Review dates, guest ages where supplied, room count, budget, preferred area and special requirements.

2. Contact suitable hotels manually. Record one or more proposed options: hotel name, location, room type, meal plan, dates, nights, rooms, price, currency, inclusions, exclusions and cancellation terms.

3. Record the time availability was checked, supplier contact, any deposit deadline, and the actual reservation or provisional-hold reference if the hotel provides one. Internal supplier notes stay private.

4. Select the agreed option for the invoice. The guide may record a provisional or confirmed reservation before invoicing if the supplier permits it; never claim a confirmed room without actual supplier confirmation.

5. Prepare and preview the invoice with the tour and agreed accommodation charges. Request a deposit or full payment and send the customer a private invoice link and notification.

6. After payment is verified, complete any outstanding supplier booking and update the hotel and tour confirmation statuses separately. Send the genuine reservation details and final arrangements to the customer.


Keep availability, finance and customer decisions separate

Record | Suggested states / control

- Tour booking: Submitted, under review, awaiting customer, awaiting payment, confirmed, completed, declined, cancelled.

- Accommodation arrangement: Requested, sourcing, proposed, held, confirmed, unavailable, declined. Store hold expiry when applicable.

- Invoice lifecycle: Draft, issued, void; retain issued versions. Settlement is tracked separately as unpaid, partially paid or paid.

- Confirmation rule: Only authorized staff confirm after checking availability, supplier agreement and the applicable payment terms. Deposit payment alone does not prove a room exists.

If no suitable hotel is available, record the outcome and ask the customer whether they accept another option. Do not silently substitute a different class, location, price or cancellation policy. Keep the original request, proposal changes and customer approval or communication history.

Use an internal supplier record only where useful for repeat bookings; a full hotel inventory, room-stock engine, supplier portal or public hotel marketplace is not required. Quotes or proformas may optionally precede the invoice, but downloadable invoices and partial/full-payment tracking are required now.


6. TRANSPORT CATALOGUE MANAGED BY ADMIN

Implement a simple standalone transport module within the same website. Customers choose from routes the administrator has explicitly published. Avoid a map-based dispatch application, arbitrary-distance fare engine or mandatory tour itinerary.

Route record | Administrator-managed fields

- Identity and coverage: Route name, slug, region, origin, destination, endpoint areas, short description and optional image.

- Journey details: Approximate duration, pickup/drop-off instructions, inclusions, exclusions and relevant luggage or accessibility notes.

- Publication: Draft/published status, active dates or simple booking cutoff where needed, display order.

- Vehicle types available: Assign only the types actually offered for this route; an active global type alone must not make it bookable on every route.

- Fares per route and type: Price, currency, pricing basis, passenger capacity, luggage allowance, any minimum/maximum party rules and explicitly listed extra charges.

- Optional return service: Off by default. Show it only if a return fare or an explicit return pricing rule is configured; otherwise create a separate reverse route.


Transport and vehicle types

Administrators can create, edit, activate and deactivate types without a code change. Examples: private car, minivan, safari 4x4, minibus, bus, speedboat and ferry. Each type has a display name, optional mode/category, description, optional image, capacity and luggage information. Examples are demo options, not claims that the operator offers them.

Use a route-to-vehicle fare record so the same route can have different fares for a private car, minivan or boat. Allow route-specific capacity and fare overrides. Keep a type disabled until required details are configured. A route must have at least one valid active fare before publication.


Simple public route cards

- Show origin to destination, estimated duration, supported vehicle types, key inclusions and a Book Transport button.

- Where a card shows From, calculate it from valid active offers and label the currency and fare basis. Show the actual chosen fare after type selection.

- Allow simple region and origin/destination filters. Only published routes and their active offers appear.

- Opposite directions may have different prices and service conditions; do not assume a published route automatically works in reverse.

- Deactivating or editing a route/type affects future requests. Preserve the details and prices already snapshotted into existing requests and issued invoices.


7. INDEPENDENT TRANSPORT BOOKING FLOW

A visitor must be able to complete this flow from the Transport menu without selecting a tour, destination package or accommodation. Keep each step short and show the exact chosen service before submission.

1. Choose a published route, then a vehicle/transport type offered on that route.

2. Enter pickup/service date and local time, adults/children or total passengers, luggage and the pickup/drop-off details required by that route.

3. Collect the lead customer's name, email, international phone number and country. Allow optional arrival flight/ferry number, accessible-travel needs and bounded special-request text.

4. Show route, direction, transport type, party size, vehicle or seat quantity, service time, fare basis, currency, included/extra charges and estimated total. If a configured return service is selected, collect its date/time and show its explicit charge.

5. Complete privacy/terms acknowledgement and anti-bot verification; submit a transport request with its own reference and confirmation page.


Capacity and pricing rules

- For a private vehicle fare, amount = configured route/type fare x vehicle count. If multiple vehicles are supported, vehicle count = ceiling(passengers / configured capacity); clearly show that quantity and price. Otherwise reject an oversized party or offer a larger supported type.

- For a per-person/per-seat fare, amount = configured route/type fare x billable passengers. Child discounts or infant rules apply only when configured and must be visible. Do not infer them.

- Validate capacities, passenger counts, luggage restrictions, direction, active offer, cutoff and service time on the backend. Never accept the client-supplied amount or a type not assigned to the route.

- Fixed route fares do not need GPS, geocoding, distance calculations, dynamic driver assignment or live fleet tracking.

- No live seat or vehicle inventory is promised. The operator reviews availability and sends an invoice/payment request before final confirmation under the applicable terms.

Demo only | Expected result

- Car: capacity 4, fare TZS 80,000 per vehicle: 5 passengers require 2 cars and TZS 160,000 only if multiple vehicles are enabled; otherwise reject that choice.

- Minivan: capacity 8, fare TZS 120,000 per vehicle: 5 passengers require 1 minivan and TZS 120,000.

- Boat/ferry: fare TZS 30,000 per person: 3 billable passengers produce TZS 90,000, subject to configured capacity and review.

Use separate transport screens, request validation and service logic; shared customer-contact fields, invoice generation, security, mail delivery and reporting utilities are acceptable. Database tour/accommodation relationships must remain optional or absent for a transport booking. Do not merge a transport request into a tour cart automatically.


8. ESTIMATES, CURRENCIES AND MONEY RULES

Calculate every payable or estimated amount on the server from current configured data. JavaScript can provide an immediate preview, but it must not decide the amount saved or invoiced.


Tour estimates

Custom tour estimate = destination charges + selected food + selected activities + selected guide services + disclosed applicable extras/taxes. Package estimates use the package's configured basis and eligible extras. Included package transport or accommodation is represented within that package's price, not added a second time. Additional guide-sourced accommodation remains Pending quotation and excluded from the calculated estimate until priced.

- Support relevant units such as per person, per group, per day and per meal, with explicit quantities and durations. Make it clear whether a displayed amount is per person or the whole group.

- A missing required price means quotation required, not zero. A genuinely free service must be explicitly configured as free.

- Prevent duplicate charging for included meals, activities, guides and transfers. Reject incompatible region/service combinations.

- If a price changes before submission, present the revised breakdown and require review instead of silently accepting a different amount.

- Store names, units, quantities, inclusions, exclusions, currency, applied rate and amount snapshots on submission and on invoicing; subsequent catalogue edits must not rewrite history.


TZS and USD

Preserve the source requirement to display Tanzanian shillings and US dollars. Use TZS and USD codes with unambiguous symbols. Each estimate, invoice and payment ledger must have one settlement currency. If a second display currency is calculated, use an administrator-maintained exchange rate with its effective date; snapshot the rate and mark the secondary amount as indicative. Never add raw TZS and USD values. Do not invent exchange rates or silently convert a payment.


Invoice and deposit calculations

Value | Definition

- Line amount: Validated quantity x unit price, applying the defined pricing unit.

- Grand total: Subtotal - configured discount + applicable configured tax. Default to no tax until the business supplies a valid setup; no invented tax rate.

- Deposit required: A percentage of grand total or fixed amount, clamped between zero and grand total; alternatively request the full total.

- Net paid / balance: Net paid = verified posted payments - recorded refunds; balance = grand total - net paid. Handle overpayments explicitly.

- Due now: For deposit mode: max(0, min(balance, deposit required - net paid)); for full mode: remaining balance. Show total, deposit requirement, already paid and balance separately.

Use MySQL DECIMAL monetary fields and decimal-safe PHP arithmetic or integer minor units; do not use floating-point arithmetic for money. Apply consistent documented rounding. Reject invalid negative amounts, percentages over 100 and unconfigured fees. Use actual date differences for accommodation nights.


9. INVOICES AND MANUAL PAYMENT RECORDING

Provide an administrator/authorized-guide invoice editor and a branded, readable A4 PDF. Invoices may cover a tour and its arranged accommodation, or a standalone transport booking. A customer receives a clear request to pay a deposit or the full amount.

Invoice content | Required fields

- Identity: Unique invoice number, booking reference, issue date, due date, company name/logo/contact details and customer billing details.

- Services: Description, service dates, quantity/unit, unit price and amount. Agreed hotel name, rooms/nights/board and route/type details where relevant.

- Totals: Currency, subtotal, disclosed discounts/taxes, grand total, deposit or full-payment request, amount paid, due now and outstanding balance.

- Payment instructions: Configured bank/mobile/manual payment details, payment reference, deadlines, cancellation/refund terms and applicable confirmation conditions.

- Delivery: PDF preview/download; explicit send action; email notification containing a private expiring link. Optional manual WhatsApp sharing of the protected link.


Document integrity

Generate invoice numbers using a transaction-safe sequence and database uniqueness, for example INV-2026-000001. Preview drafts before sending. Freeze the contents of issued invoices; keep a revision or void/reissue history with links to superseded documents. Preserve existing payment links and audit history. Do not delete issued or paid records through normal CRUD actions. A quotation/proforma is optional and must be labelled separately from an invoice or a receipt.


Deposit and full payments without a gateway

1. The customer follows the invoice's configured offline/manual payment instructions. No card or wallet checkout is required in the initial implementation.

2. Authorized staff verify money received and record amount, currency, date, method, transaction reference and recorder. A screenshot or customer assertion alone must not mark the payment verified.

3. Record partial payments, final payments and any refunds as separate ledger entries. Prevent duplicate recording of the same transaction where identifiable and use transactional updates.

4. Recalculate net paid, due now and balance; show unpaid, partially paid or paid settlement status. Provide a payment acknowledgement/receipt after verified payment.

5. Update booking and supplier confirmation only through the applicable authorized workflow, independently of invoice settlement.

Keep invoice PDFs and any optional payment evidence in private storage. Customer access requires an unguessable, expiring, revocable token or appropriately protected temporary signed link; knowing an invoice number must never be enough. Store only token hashes and scope access to the intended document. Superseded or void links must cease granting access to the active document. Do not put personal details in URLs, public storage paths, sitemaps or analytics.

11. BOOKING SECURITY AND ABUSE PREVENTION

Security is a working backend requirement. A CAPTCHA widget alone does not make a booking endpoint secure. Protect tour submissions, transport submissions, contact forms and sensitive staff/customer-document actions.


Cloudflare Turnstile as the default CAPTCHA alternative

- Use a Managed Turnstile widget on the final submit stage of public booking/contact forms. A provider abstraction may permit reCAPTCHA later; do not require customers to solve two providers.

- Store the site key as public configuration and the secret key in environment variables. Enforce the production hostname allowlist and a distinct expected action for tour_booking, transport_booking and contact.

- Verify the token from Laravel through the provider's Siteverify endpoint. Require success and check the returned hostname and action against the expected values. Client-side callbacks alone must never authorize a booking.

- Turnstile tokens expire after five minutes and are single-use. Generate or refresh verification near submission, reset after expiry/rejection and preserve the customer's form values.

- If verification times out or fails, explain the retry and reject the new submission; do not silently bypass it. Use official test keys or a mocked verifier only in isolated local/test environments. Production startup must detect missing keys or an enabled bypass.


Defence in depth

- Use Laravel CSRF protection for state-changing forms, FormRequest validation, Eloquent/parameter binding and explicit allowed fields. Validate IDs, region compatibility, quantities, currency, route/type assignments and all current prices server-side.

- Apply configurable Laravel rate limits to bookings, contact, login, password reset, estimate endpoints and document-access attempts. Combine appropriate IP/session or hashed-identity limits; return a useful retry message. Trust proxy headers only from configured proxies.

- Use an accessible honeypot as an additional signal, not a replacement for verification. Limit text lengths and request sizes. Scope idempotency keys to the guest session and payload, use database uniqueness and return the existing request on a genuine retry instead of creating duplicate bookings or emails.

- Require HTTPS, secure HttpOnly session cookies, suitable SameSite settings, session rotation on login, password hashing and throttled authentication. Send staff directly to the dashboard after successful password login and authorize every record action through policies.

- Escape untrusted output in Blade; sanitize any permitted rich text. Apply security headers and a CSP compatible with Turnstile. Keep debug output and secrets out of production.

- Check uploaded images by actual type, extension, size and dimensions; allow only approved formats, use generated names and block executable content. Store financial evidence privately. Serve private downloads through authorization/token checks; prevent path traversal and insecure direct object access.

CAPTCHA, CSRF, rate limiting, authorization and price validation solve different problems and must coexist. Test forged, expired, replayed and wrong-action tokens as well as price manipulation and unauthorized invoice access.


12. DATA MODEL, CODE STRUCTURE AND DEPLOYMENT

Design the ERD and migration constraints before building screens. Use practical Laravel models, controllers, services, policies, FormRequests, jobs and reusable Blade components. Do not replace the requested stack with a JavaScript SPA or a different database.

Data group | Suggested tables / relationships

- Staff and content: users, roles/permissions as needed; regions, destinations, destination_images; tours, tour_itineraries, tour_destination and package inclusion records.

- Selectable services: activities, food_options, travel_guides and suitable region/destination relationships.

- Requests: bookings with kind tour or transport and tour source package/custom; booking_items with immutable descriptive/price snapshots; optional trip_plans/items for the planner.

- Tour / accommodation: tour_booking_details; accommodation_requests belongs to a tour booking; accommodation_proposals and a selected proposal reference; preference lookup records. No public hotel inventory is required.

- Independent transport: transport_routes, vehicle_types, transport_route_fares (route x type), transport_booking_details belongs to a transport booking and selected fare; snapshot route, type, capacity, times and prices.

- Finance and delivery: invoices, invoice_items, invoice revisions or immutable snapshots, payments/refunds, document_sequences, private access tokens, notification_outbox/delivery attempts and audit_logs.

- CMS / operations: galleries/media, pages, site_settings, inquiries; sessions, cache, jobs and failed_jobs as needed.

Use foreign keys, unique booking/invoice references, scoped idempotency keys and indexed booking kind/status/dates. Preserve historical snapshots when source content is edited or deactivated. Enforce that accommodation belongs only to tour requests and that transport requests do not require tour IDs. Store instants in UTC, interpret service dates/times using Africa/Dar_es_Salaam, and keep accommodation dates as local calendar dates.


Deployment and operation

- Use service classes such as TourPricingService, TransportPricingService, BookingSubmissionService, AccommodationArrangementService, InvoiceService and CaptchaVerifier. Keep controllers thin and policy checks explicit.

- Choose a maintained server-side PDF dependency compatible with the chosen Laravel/PHP versions. Use controlled local assets and disable unneeded remote resource fetching in invoice rendering.

- Provide migrations, demo seeders, environment examples without secrets and lockfiles. Use a persistent database queue initially; Redis is optional, not required.

- Document PHP extensions, Composer, MySQL, asset build steps, storage permissions, public/ document root, HTTPS, cron/scheduler and queue processing. Node is needed for building assets; no persistent Node server is required.

- On a VPS use a supervised worker; on a compatible shared host document a cron-based queue worker with overlap prevention. Set APP_DEBUG=false, cache production config appropriately, protect .env and test backup restoration.


13. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA AND IMPLEMENTATION DELIVERY

The implementation is complete only when the principal customer and administrator workflows work against the database. Use automated tests for pricing, persistence, authorization and security, plus browser checks for the full mobile/desktop journeys.

1. Navigation has Transport and exactly five categories in each regional dropdown. No public accommodation menu, hotel list or direct hotel booking is exposed.

2. Published tours and the custom planner support their configured units, dates and region rules. Included services are not charged twice; missing prices show quotation required.

3. A customer completes a tour without accommodation. Another requests accommodation at the end, including a stay before or after the tour; conditional validation works and the hotel price remains pending.

4. A guide reviews preferences, records a real proposed hotel and reservation/hold status, selects the agreed offer and prepares an invoice. Internal notes remain private.

5. An admin creates a new vehicle type and route fare; it appears without a code change. A customer books that route/type without any tour or accommodation record.

6. Incompatible types, unpublished routes, invalid times and capacity overflow are rejected. Per-person and per-vehicle calculations, multiple-vehicle quantities and configured return charges are correct.

7. Manipulated client prices, cross-region IDs and changed catalogue prices are handled safely. Currency conversion is explicit; snapshots and issued invoices survive catalogue changes.
